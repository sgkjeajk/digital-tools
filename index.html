Stocks · ETFs · Market monitoring

Stock / ETF Monitor

Your watchlist · Clear price changes · One organised view

Keep your watchlist
in clear view.

Follow the stocks and ETFs that matter to you without jumping between individual quote pages. Stock / ETF Monitor brings saved market prices, daily percentage changes and drawdown from recorded highs into one organised dashboard.

Track US-listed instruments and supported international tickers, including Singapore’s STI ETF, with each instrument’s currency and market timestamp clearly shown.

Open Stock / ETF Monitor ↗
01 · An organised watchlist

Your stocks and ETFs. Together.

Keep the instruments you follow in one place, with ETFs grouped first and tickers sorted alphabetically for easy scanning.

  • Separate ETF and stock tables
  • Names, markets and currencies
  • Desktop and mobile access
02 · Changes with context

See the price. Understand the change.

Compare saved current prices with the previous close and see how far each instrument sits below its recorded historical high.

  • Previous close and daily changes
  • Current price and percentage change
  • Recorded high and drawdown percentage
03 · A list you can manage

Keep your monitoring relevant.

Add, edit, disable or remove tickers through Admin. Saved changes reach the Monitor on its next refresh, with scheduled updates supplying market data.

  • Automatic ticker names and classification
  • Shared configuration with Admin
  • Visible refresh and market timestamps

About the project

Useful technology, made easier to use.

This independent collection brings together practical digital tools for learning, daily living, active ageing, accessibility and established exercise routines. The aim is simple: make useful digital experiences clearer and more approachable for different generations.

New categories can be added over time, including caregiver tools, accessibility tools, health-information tools, community resources and additional educational games.

Safety & privacy

Minimal data by design

No account is required to browse this portal. The request form asks only for information needed to understand and respond to a request.

When connected, the request form sends your details through Formspree so the website owner can reply. External tools have their own privacy practices.

Exercise content is informational support for following established instructions, not medical diagnosis, treatment or personalised medical advice.
