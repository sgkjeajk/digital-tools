Lower Back & Hip Mobility

Safety first

These exercises help to improve Lower Back & Hip Mobility. This app is an animated guide. It does not replace medical or physiotherapy advice. Please follow the instructions your physiotherapist or doctor gave you personally.

Stop if an exercise causes pain, dizziness, unusual symptoms or significant discomfort. These exercises are not a treatment or cure for any condition.

This is an unofficial app. It is not made or checked by NUH.

This viewer is not saving your data. Your settings and history will be lost when you close it. Open the file in Safari, Chrome or Edge to keep them. You can also copy a backup from History.
